1 District Education Officer 07652-245032
2 Assistant Commissioner Tribal Work Department 07652-222895

Being a tribal district, a majority of schools are governed by the Tribal Affairs Department. Schools are categorised into primary, middle, high school and higher secondary school. Primary and middle schools are managed by the concerned education authorities, while other schools are managed by their respective departments. Examinations and educational activities are conducted according to government guidelines.

The Education Department works towards improving educational facilities, student development, school infrastructure and the quality of education throughout Shahdol District.
